# RecallGraph feedback survey brief

Submit feedback through the [RecallGraph product feedback form](https://github.com/ajanaku1/RecallGraph/issues/new?template=feedback.yml). The form is also recorded in `submission/manifest.json` and checked by the release verifier.

## Questions

1. Which role best describes you: model risk, data governance, ML engineering, platform engineering, security, or other?
2. Could you identify why the recall was blocked before approval?
3. Did the lineage view make the affected models and deployment clear?
4. Was the difference between live DataHub evidence and fixture evidence clear?
5. Did the integrity-receipt disclaimer explain what SHA-256 does and does not prove?
6. Which missing integration would matter most for production use?
7. What part of the recall workflow was unclear?
8. Would you use RecallGraph during a real data or model incident?

The form does not ask for an email or other personal details. A GitHub account is required to submit feedback.
