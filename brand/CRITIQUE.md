# RecallGraph closure stitch critique: Checkpoint B

## 1. Linear Stitch: Grade A

- Concept fit (A): The open left-to-right trace, narrow aperture, reconvergence, and centered cross-stitch make governed closure immediately legible.
- Brand compliance (A): It uses only graphite and vermilion, contains no letters or category cliché, and reserves the accent for the seal crossing the terminal.
- Craft (A): The seal has balanced overhang on both sides of the graphite terminal and a clean, deliberate weight contrast.
- Legibility (A): At 16px it reads as a single exposed trace decisively crossed at closure, not a detached end stop.
- Ownability (B): The visible act of stitching is specific and repeatable, though its linearity needs clear-space discipline in future layouts.

Verdict: The idea is right; any future terminal-spacing refinement is execution-level.

## 2. Fold Stitch: Grade B

- Concept fit (B): The descending diagonal fold and brief diamond-like aperture communicate a compact trace being gathered before it is sealed.
- Brand compliance (A): The revised angular construction avoids the former J/fishhook reading as well as R, U, checkmark, chain, and database silhouettes.
- Craft (B): The measured fold and horizontal crossing are precise, though the inner aperture has less breathing room than the Linear Stitch at larger display sizes.
- Legibility (B): Its vertical start and diagonal fold remain recognizable at 16px. The aperture condenses into a purposeful notch instead of a letterform.
- Ownability (B): The folded trace is distinct from a rotated linear mark, but its more complex route makes it a less immediate recurring symbol.

Verdict: The direction is viable; aperture spacing is an execution-level refinement, not a structural failure.

## 3. Orbit Stitch: Grade B

- Concept fit (B): The two incomplete, skewed trajectories gathering toward one diagonal terminal suggest reconciling downstream consequence without literal graph imagery.
- Brand compliance (A): The mark has no C/G, eye, power-button, shield, or circular-app-icon silhouette because its paired paths travel only as a partial diagonal sweep.
- Craft (B): The tracked paths taper into a clear common terminal and the angled seal crosses it cleanly, though the initial path spacing could be optically tuned.
- Legibility (B): At 16px the paired sweep simplifies to an asymmetric motion mark with a still-visible angled closure action.
- Ownability (B): Its restrained double trajectory is distinctive, but it is less explicit about trace direction than the Linear Stitch.

Verdict: The idea is compliant; its comparatively abstract trace reading is a structural trade-off, not a forbidden resemblance.

## Ranking and recommendation

1. **Linear Stitch**
2. **Fold Stitch**
3. **Orbit Stitch**

Recommendation: advance **Linear Stitch** to the human selection checkpoint; refine only its execution-level terminal spacing after a direction is chosen.
