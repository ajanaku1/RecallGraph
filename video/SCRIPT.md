# RecallGraph demo narration

Voice: Microsoft Edge TTS `en-US-AndrewNeural`, rate `+5%`.

## 1. Hook

A training dataset loses its license after models are already in production. Which models must be recalled? Who approved each response? And how do you prove nothing was missed?

## 2. Problem and positioning

Most catalogs can show lineage, but an incident still closes through screenshots, tickets, and memory. RecallGraph turns that gap into a deterministic model recall command: trace every affected descendant, require human dispositions, and fail closed until the evidence is complete.

## 3. Real product reveal

This case starts with a revoked source. The normalized graph reaches two models and one production deployment. Both model decisions are unresolved, so closure and writeback are blocked. The hosted console is clearly labeled fixture evidence; the workflow is deterministic and repeatable for every judge.

## 4. Judge moment: governed closure

Approving ranking V two is not enough. RecallGraph binds each approval to the current case revision, so changed evidence makes stale approvals invalid. After both reviewed dispositions are recorded, a server-issued writeback reference unlocks the guarded close action.

## 5. Judge moment: integrity receipt

Closing the case exports the complete lineage, event, decisions, approvals, uncertainty resolutions, and writeback evidence as canonical JSON. SHA two fifty-six produces a stable integrity digest. A trusted match passes. Plant one mismatch, and verification fails without pretending the checksum is a signature.

## 6. Verifiable DataHub proof

The sponsor integration is real and separate from the hosted fixture. Against DataHub O S S, M C P server DataHub returned two affected M L models and one downstream process. RecallGraph then wrote a probe metadata property, confirmed readback, and restored the original value. The retained evidence records every U R N.

## 7. Close

RecallGraph is public under Apache two point zero, deployed on Vercel, and covered by one hundred automated checks plus a green production build. Every affected model, traced and resolved. Open the live recall command or inspect the repository.
